HTML----How To Make Lemonade

Ingredients
    To make lemonade, you will need ingredients in next page(progressing).
  • Fresh lemons
  • Water
  • Sugar
  • Ice (optional)

Instructions

  1. Squeeze the juice from the lemons into a measuring cup.
  2. Add water to the lemon juice.
  3. Add sugar to taste and stir until dissolved.
  4. Serve over ice if desired.

Tips

  • For a sweeter lemonade, add more sugar.
  • For a tart lemonade, add more lemon juice.
  • Experiment with different flavors by adding mint or berries.

Variations

  • Strawberry Lemonade: Blend strawberries with lemon juice and water.
  • Mint Lemonade: Add fresh mint leaves to the lemonade.
  • Spicy Lemonade: Add a pinch of cayenne pepper for a kick.

Enjoy your lemonade!

Feel free to adjust the sweetness and tartness to your liking!
