Primary Colors (60%):

Sunny Lemon Yellow: #FFE45C (A warm yellow similar to fresh lemons)

Soft Light Beige: #FFF9E6 (Used as a background transition color to reduce visual fatigue)

Secondary Colors (30%):

Lime Leaf Green: #8BC34A (Evokes the fresh, natural feel of lemon leaves)

Citrus Orange: #FFA726 (Used for emphasis on action buttons and icons)

Wood Brown: #BCAAA4 (Used to accent wooden elements)

Accent Colors (10%):

Vibrant Coral Red: #FF7043 (Used for important notifications and call-to-action buttons)

Neutral Colors:

Light Gray: #F5F5F5 (Background support color)

Medium Gray: #757575 (Secondary text)

Dark Gray: #424242 (Primary text)

Application Recommendations:

Layering Design:

Use #FFF9E6 as the main background

Titles with a gradient of #FFE45C (recommended gradient direction: left to right, 80% → 100% transparency)

Buttons with a subtle gradient from #FFA726 to #FF7043

Contrast Control:

Maintain a 7:1 contrast ratio between text and background

Important information should use #FF7043 with white text

Action buttons should have a 1px border in #BCAAA4 for better recognition

Visual Flow:

Use light shadows in focal areas (recommended: rgba(255,228,92,0.2))

Dividers should be thin lines with 30% transparency of #8BC34A

Adaptation Plan:

For dark mode, adjust the primary color to #F9D71C (reduce brightness)

Adjust secondary colors to #689F38 and #E65100 accordingly

Design Considerations:

Avoid using pure white backgrounds extensively; use #FFFDF5 as the base background color

When overlaying images, use a linear gradient (90deg, #FFE45C20, #8BC34A10)

For charts, apply primary colors with different brightness levels within the same color scheme
